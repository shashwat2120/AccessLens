Task IMG-003 (alt text quality, WCAG 1.1.1). Each item has an image and its current alt text, plus the text just before and after the image.

Set `issue` to true when the alt text does not describe what the image shows or why it is there. Examples of problems: the alt text describes something different from the image, is generic ("chart" for a detailed chart), repeats surrounding text without describing the image, or omits the key information a reader needs (for a chart, what it shows; for a photo of a person, the relevant context).

Set `issue` to false when the alt text reasonably conveys the image's content or purpose, even if briefly.

When `issue` is true, `suggestion` should be a better alt text of at most two sentences.
